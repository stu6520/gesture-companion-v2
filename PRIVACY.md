# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 22, 2026

Gesture Companion does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, artwork, touch data, or usage analytics.

## Local data

Gesture Companion stores gesture preferences locally on the user's device at:

`%APPDATA%\GestureCompanion\pointer-host-settings.json`

For troubleshooting, Gesture Companion writes a local diagnostic log at:

`%LOCALAPPDATA%\GestureCompanion\bridge.log`

The log contains technical information such as process names, process identifiers, bridge status, gesture counters, and error messages. It is not transmitted automatically. A user may choose to share this log when requesting support.

## Application interaction

Gesture Companion uses a local in-process bridge with supported drawing applications to receive touch messages and deliver user-configured input commands. This processing occurs locally on the user's device.

## Network access

Gesture Companion does not require an internet connection and does not contain built-in telemetry, advertising, analytics, account tracking, or cloud synchronization.

Microsoft Store installation, licensing, and updates are provided by Microsoft and are subject to Microsoft's own privacy terms.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be published in this repository.

## Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be submitted through the repository's GitHub Issues page.
